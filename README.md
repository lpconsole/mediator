## About MediaTor

**MediaTor** is a fast, multilingual social news platform that aggregates verified national and international news agencies in a single transparent feed—free from anonymous rumor accounts and algorithmic manipulation.

This repository hosts official **Android APK releases** for users who want a native mobile experience with instant updates and smooth navigation.

---

## Key Features

- **Verified News Agencies:** Articles originate exclusively from vetted publisher channels and official feeds with full source attribution and cryptographic verification imprints.
- **8-Language Global Support:** Full interface localization in **Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Azerbaijani, and Arabic (RTL)**.
- **Personalized Feed & Channels:** Follow your favorite news agencies, filter by country and category, or explore breaking, popular, and most-discussed stories.
- **Open Civic Community:** Comment on stories, share source-backed perspectives, bookmark articles, and join live discussions.
- **Smart Search & Widgets:** Fast unified search across stories, publisher channels, and live trends.
- **Light & Dark Theme:** Automatic system theme sync with crisp light and OLED-friendly dark modes.

## Download & Installation (Android APK)

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/lpconsole/mediator-android/releases/latest)** page.
2. Download the latest `MediaTor-vX.X.X.apk` file to your Android device.
3. Open the downloaded file and allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.
4. Launch **MediaTor** and sign in or create a free account.
